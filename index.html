🧑 Career Summary

I am a machine learning researcher and an M.Sc. candidate in Computer Science at İstanbul Technical University (İTÜ), where I am a Research Assistant in the Multimedia Processing Lab. My research focuses on reliable and generalizable machine learning, with interests in deep learning, large language models, and vision–language models, particularly their reasoning, interpretability, and robustness. My thesis, Grounding and Trust in Joint-Embedding Predictive World Models: Identifiability Limits and Calibrated Prediction Horizons, is advised by Prof. Dr. Uluğ Bayazıt. I am also the founder of the Casual Language and Multimodal Intelligence Lab.

My work spans three interconnected directions:

  1. World Models and Calibrated Prediction: representation identifiability and predictive reliability in joint-embedding predictive architectures, action-conditioned world models, and uncertainty calibration of prediction horizons under distribution shift.
  2. Reliable and Interpretable Language Models: reasoning and faithfulness evaluation of LLMs, reliability auditing of NLP classifiers, and hidden-state and layer-wise analysis of adversarial intent.
  3. Multimodal and Embodied Reasoning: typed causal scene graphs for counterfactual physical reasoning, reliability-guided depth completion, and sparse cross-modal routing for multimodal question answering.

I work with Dr. Sharifa Sultana (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign), Dr. Mohammad Ali Moni (University of Queensland), and Prof. Dr. Uluğ Bayazıt (İTÜ). Previously, I was a Research Intern at the İTÜ AI and Data Science Research Center (summer 2025) and at TÜBİTAK (summer 2024).

My work has resulted in accepted workshop papers at ACL, EMNLP, ICML, NeurIPS, and CVPR 2026 workshops, with journal manuscripts under review at IJCV, TMLR, and Expert Systems with Applications and four manuscripts under review at ICLR 2027. I received the ICML 2026 Gold Reviewer Award (top 25% of reviewers), reviewed for TMLR, Springer Nature Multimedia Systems, IEEE IoT, and major ML conferences, and have mentored 10 undergraduate students, four of whose projects became workshop publications. I am a recipient of the fully funded Türkiye Bursları graduate scholarship and volunteer as a science communicator with Biggani.org.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027. Feel free to email me (islam23 at itu dot edu dot tr) or connect via the links below if you are interested in collaboration or discussing research.
